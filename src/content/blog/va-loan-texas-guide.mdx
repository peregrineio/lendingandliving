If you're a veteran, active-duty service member, or surviving spouse in Texas, you may hold one of the strongest home-buying benefits that exists: the **VA loan**. No down payment, no monthly mortgage insurance, and competitive rates. Here's how it works and how to find out if you qualify.

## What Is a VA Loan?

A [VA loan](/services/va-loans-houston) is a mortgage benefit backed by the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs. The VA doesn't lend the money — it guarantees a portion of the loan, which lets VA-approved lenders offer terms no other program matches.

## The Key Benefits

- **No down payment** — finance 100% of the purchase price
- **No monthly mortgage insurance** — a major saving versus FHA or low-down conventional
- **Competitive rates** — often among the best available
- **Flexible credit** — most lenders work with scores around 580–620
- **Limited closing costs** — the VA caps certain fees
- **No prepayment penalty**

## Who's Eligible in Texas?

You may qualify if you meet one of these service thresholds:

- **Active duty:** 90 continuous days during wartime, or 181 during peacetime
- **Veterans:** minimum service requirements based on your service era
- **National Guard / Reserves:** 6 years of service, or 90 days of qualifying active duty
- **Surviving spouses:** unmarried spouses of veterans who died in service or from a service-connected disability

Eligibility is confirmed with a **Certificate of Eligibility (COE)** — which I can usually pull for you in minutes.

## The VA Funding Fee

VA loans carry a one-time **funding fee** that sustains the program — currently 2.15% of the loan for first-time use with no down payment, and it can be rolled into the loan. Importantly, **veterans receiving VA disability compensation are typically exempt** from the fee entirely.

## Can You Use It More Than Once?

Yes — VA eligibility is a lifetime, reusable benefit. Many Texas veterans use it for every home they buy, as long as entitlement is available or restored.

## VA vs. FHA vs. Conventional

For eligible buyers, VA is very hard to beat: no down payment and no monthly mortgage insurance, where [FHA](/services/fha-loans-houston) requires both. The rare exceptions are properties that don't meet VA appraisal standards — worth a conversation, not an assumption.
