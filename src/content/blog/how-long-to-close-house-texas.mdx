"How long does closing take?" is one of the most common questions I get. In Texas the answer depends on your loan type, how fast you respond to document requests, and how the inspection and appraisal go. Here's what to realistically expect.

## Average Closing Timelines in Texas

Most Texas purchases close within **30 to 45 days** of a signed contract:

- **[Conventional loans](/services/conventional-loans-houston):** 30–45 days
- **[FHA loans](/services/fha-loans-houston):** 30–45 days (occasionally slightly longer)
- **[VA loans](/services/va-loans-houston):** roughly 40–50 days
- **USDA loans:** 45–60 days (extra USDA review step)
- **Cash purchases:** as fast as 7–14 days

## The Option Period — Texas's Built-In Timeline Step

Texas contracts include an **option period** (usually 5–10 negotiated days) when the buyer can exit for any reason. It's when inspections and repair negotiations happen, and it's baked into the overall timeline — plan for it. The full play-by-play is in my [offer-to-closing guide](/blog/offer-accepted-to-closing-texas).

## What Slows Closings Down

- **Appraisal scheduling** in busy seasons
- **Title issues** — liens, unpaid taxes, ownership disputes
- **Underwriting conditions** answered slowly
- **Repair negotiations** that run long
- **Lender backlogs** during peak buying months

## How to Speed Yours Up

1. Get fully **pre-approved** before offering — not just pre-qualified
2. Respond to every lender request within 24 hours
3. Make **no big financial changes** mid-process — no new credit, no unexplained large deposits, no job changes without talking to your loan officer first
4. Work with a loan officer who anticipates problems instead of reacting to them
5. Have your documents ready up front — tax returns, pay stubs, bank statements

## What If You Miss the Closing Date?

You negotiate an extension with the seller. Most sellers grant a few days for a legitimate reason, but some contracts carry per-day costs — one more reason staying responsive matters.

## Can You Close Faster Than 30 Days?

Sometimes — well-prepared buyers with responsive lenders do close in the low 20s or faster. It's never a promise, but preparation dramatically improves the odds.
